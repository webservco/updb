--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
<h3>What's the big idea?</h3>

<p>We want to build a global centralized database of underground pubications (fanzines, books, newspapers).</p>

<h3>I can already download zines on this site and that site</h3>

<p>Unlike other websites where you can download and read fanzines, the UPDb's goal is not have downloadable scanned fanzines, but instead to provide a realiable source of information about these publications, and allow users to share this information, along with trading publications between them.</p>

<h3>What's innit for me?</h3>

<p>You can register an account and start organizing your zine or book collection.</p>
<p>You can also manage and share you wantlists and tradelists.</p>

<h3>I want to add zines to the database!</h3>

<p>Since the database structure is still under developent we are not accepting external contributors at the moment.</p>

<p>But if you want to contribute information to the database, please get in touch and we will add it.</p>
